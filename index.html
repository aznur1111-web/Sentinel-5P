Interpretation boundary

Satellite trace-gas columns are affected by altitude, clouds, retrieval assumptions, wind and chemistry. They do not alone identify individual emitters or establish ground-level exposure or causality.

Data provenance requirements

For every numeric record, store source URL, product identifier, acquisition time, units, retrieval version, quality flags, averaging method, spatial footprint, and whether it is measured, modeled or synthetic. Keep these distinctions in all exports.
